Readings and backups

Readings and notes are stored on your device. You can export a JSON backup from Past readings and import it later. Exported files are plaintext, so choose a location you trust. Save an unfinished note before exporting; drafts are not included in a history backup. Uninstalling the app may remove readings that you have not backed up.

E-iching version 1.0 is free and does not require an account.
