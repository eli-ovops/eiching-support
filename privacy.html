Privacy policy

Effective September 24, 2026. E-iching is operated by eli. For privacy questions, email mfl2706ae@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Your optional question, chosen topic, six-line reading, notes, tags, revisit date, and unfinished note drafts are stored in the app on your device. Language and sound preferences are also stored locally. E-iching has no account, app-operated cloud sync, advertising tracking, or third-party analytics. The operator does not receive your readings through the app.

Backups and deletion

Your device's system backup may include app data, depending on your settings. If you choose to export a history backup, the JSON file is plaintext. A file location or cloud service you choose may handle that file under its own policies. Unfinished note drafts are not included in a history backup; save the note first if you want it included.

You can delete a reading in Past readings. Deleting it in the app does not delete earlier exported files or system backups; manage those separately. Uninstalling the app may remove local data that was not backed up. We do not keep server copies of readings and cannot restore unbacked-up entries.

If you contact support

We use the email and information you voluntarily send only to reply to your request. The email provider processes and stores those messages as part of providing email service. You may request deletion of your support email by writing to mfl2706ae@gmail.com; we will delete the identified message from our mailbox. Copies in the provider's backups may remain according to its own retention practices.

This website

These pages use no analytics scripts or contact forms. GitHub Pages serves this website and may process technical access information under GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

We will update this page if the app's data handling changes. Apple App Store services and device backups are governed by their respective providers' policies.
